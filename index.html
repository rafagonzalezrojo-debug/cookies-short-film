SYNOPSIS

“COOKIES” plays with the idea that not everything that glitters is gold: neither a cookie, nor an elderly woman, nor seemingly trivial decisions.

A noir and satirical nod to how everyday acts, like accepting “cookies,” can hide secrets, and how every choice, no matter how small, can have unexpected and darkly amusing consequences.

DIRECTOR'S STATEMENT

My fascination lies in the small moral lessons that endure and evolve over time, those capsules of wisdom that hide great truths. COOKIES is the direct result of this, a story that takes the classic crime investigation of noir films and refracts it through our modern, often passive, habit of accepting everything in the digital realm.

In an increasingly screen-mediated world, this film serves as a necessary pause — an invitation for the audience to reflect on the small, critical decisions we make in our virtual lives before blindly clicking “accept”. The narrative is transplanted to a medieval fantasy realm, heavily influenced by the witty authenticity of Terry Pratchett’s Discworld. This setting allows the intertwining of magic and modern relevance in a way that has always obsessed me.

Developed entirely by a single person, COOKIES is a hybrid experiment: combining tactile stop-motion miniatures for tangible, immersive environments with 2D characters to convey raw emotion and narrative magic.

This technique elevates textures, lighting, and environment to the level of main characters, reinforcing the hand-crafted nature of the film. My goal is to revitalize the potential of 2D and stop-motion, proving that handcrafted techniques and miniatures can convey a tangible reality more resonant than any faithful replication.

Ultimately, I hope the audience leaves with a clear, subtle awareness: not everything that glitters is gold, and the next time they click “accept,” they remember this story and the worlds it contains.

Rafael González
Director & Creator
